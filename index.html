Cart taxes & charges
Manage percentage charges displayed automatically in the customer cart.
Connect the admin API to load taxes.

Default charges: Shipping 2%, Packaging 3%, Platform fee 1%, Delivery charges 2%, GST 18%. GST rates in India vary by product category (commonly 0%, 5%, 12%, 18% or 28%); configure the legally applicable rate for your products.

Customer discounts
Publish a percentage discount for every customer or target one customer by their account email.
Connect the admin API to load discounts.
Use the email associated with the customer’s Shoppy account.

The highest matching discount is applied once to the cart subtotal before percentage charges. A user-specific offer is matched by the signed-in account email.
